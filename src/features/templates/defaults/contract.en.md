## Responsibilities of {brand}

1. {brand} is responsible for providing the agreed space, advertising, answering customers, registration, collecting payments and coordinating the workshop.

## Responsibilities of the instructor and quality of materials

1. The instructor is responsible for preparing the teaching content, teaching, and supplying and transporting all materials and tools the workshop needs. The instructor must be at the venue before the workshop starts and have everything ready so the workshop starts on time.

2. The list, specifications and quantity of materials for each participant must be approved in writing by {brand} before advertising begins.

3. Materials and tools must be of good quality, intact, clean, hygienic and suitable for the activity and the participants' age, and, where relevant, within their expiry date.

4. Replacing approved materials requires the written consent of {brand}. Making up any shortage or replacing unsuitable items before the workshop starts is the instructor's responsibility.

## Joint advertising

1. Both {brand} and the instructor must publish the workshop information on their own pages, naming the other party, linking to its page and giving the registration path through {brand}.

2. Published information must match the agreed workshop details and terms. Advertising does not guarantee any number of registrations.

## Registration and payments

1. All registrations and payments for this workshop go through {brand}, regardless of which page the participant came from.

2. The instructor must refer registration requests to {brand} and must not take any payment directly from participants for this workshop.

3. Any change to the selling price, discounts, capacity or announced services is possible only in coordination with, and with the written approval of, {brand}.

## Fee and settlement

1. Any additional cost may be paid only with the prior written approval of {brand}.

2. Increasing the number of participants after the go / no-go decision time ({decision_deadline}) requires the instructor's approval.

3. If the workshop takes place, a participant's absence or cancellation after the final number has been announced does not reduce the amount for that participant.

4. {brand} informs the instructor of the number of confirmed registrations and the amount payable.

## Minimum registrations and cancellation by {brand}

1. Before the workshop is confirmed, the instructor may buy materials only with the written approval of {brand}.

2. If {brand} cancels the workshop, no teaching fee is paid.

3. Items paid for by {brand} must be handed over to {brand}.

4. The instructor's advance payment is set off against the costs payable; any remaining balance is returned to {brand} within five business days.

5. Refunding participants is the responsibility of {brand}.

## Cancellation by the instructor

1. If the instructor cannot hold the workshop, they must inform {brand} in writing as soon as possible.

2. A change of date or a replacement instructor is possible only with the written approval of {brand}.

3. If the workshop does not take place because the instructor cancelled, the instructor receives nothing for teaching or materials and must return the full advance payment to {brand} within five business days.

4. In case of documented illness or a serious incident beyond the instructor's control, the parties first look for a new date. If no new date is agreed, the workshop is cancelled and the advance payment is returned; each party bears its own costs.

5. Participants are refunded through {brand}.

## Participant data, photos and videos

1. Participant information given to the instructor to run the workshop is used only for that purpose. The instructor may not use it for personal promotion or pass it on to anyone else.

2. Either party may publish identifiable photos or videos of participants only in line with the consent recorded at registration.

3. Attending the workshop is not consent to publication of photos or videos.

4. Both parties may use the other party's name, page and approved promotional content to promote this workshop.

## Term and changes

1. This contract covers only the workshop specified in it. Terms for later workshops must be agreed separately.

2. Any change to the date, obligations, amount or other terms of this contract must be recorded in writing and approved by both parties.
